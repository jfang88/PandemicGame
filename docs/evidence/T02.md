## Implemented boundary

`@pandemic/engine` exports versioned readonly state/config/card types, `loadBoard`,
`createGame(config, seed)`, `nextUint32`, `randomIndex`, `shuffle` and
`assertGameInvariants`. It performs no network, database, clock or ambient-random calls.
The seed is explicitly supplied by the future server; it must be an integer from
1 through 4294967295. Zero is rejected in every RNG/setup entry point.

The canonical JSON board is imported directly, not duplicated or regenerated.
Board inputs are checked against grid48-v1's identities, labels, coordinates, colors
and edges, then copied and ordered by ID. Player inputs are copied and sorted by
stable seat (including sparse seats), with unique opaque UID/seat/role checks.

Setup uses one persistent random stream for infection shuffle, player shuffle and
four epidemic-pile shuffles. Discard top is index 0. Epidemic e:1 is appended to pile1
before its shuffle, through e:4/pile4; RULES.md now makes this previously unspecified
insertion position explicit so independent implementations reproduce the same vectors.

The game snapshot is internal: no public projection or frontend route exposes decks
or RNG. Room revisions/authentication/persistence are outside this task.
