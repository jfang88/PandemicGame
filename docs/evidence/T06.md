# T06 engine gate

T05 merged in [PR #26](https://github.com/jfang88/PandemicGame/pull/26). T06 merged in [PR #27](https://github.com/jfang88/PandemicGame/pull/27).

The engine enumerates legal actions for the current actor or pending discard owner. Each distinct set of payment cards appears once in hand order. The exploration harness selects from those choices with a separate seeded RNG, freezes inputs, checks every returned snapshot, probes wrong-actor and malformed-command rejection, and replays each full command trace against a freshly initialized game. Replay checks the state and ordered events after every command.

The trace contains the actor and command for each attempted step, including the failing step.

## Independent rules review

A separate stronger-model reviewer checked the engine against RULES.md and ACCEPTANCE.md. The review identified missing checks for intermediate phases, inconsistent terminal outcomes, and incorrect continuation ownership. T06 adds those checks and covers loss during a resumed draw, both normal and epidemic outbreak termination, epidemic supply exhaustion and the infection-rate cap. The reviewer reran the final unit suite and engine lint and typecheck and found no blockers. Failure-trace persistence was reviewed in code.

## Scope

Balance remains provisional. The random driver samples legal actions without a cooperative strategy. Targeted fixtures verify victory and the exploration results describe only the sampled traces. V1 omits undo, early turn ending, taking cards, event cards, center relocation, eradication and passive Medic effects. Storage, multiplayer and deployment have separate gates.
